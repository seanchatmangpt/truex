# Paper 85 Summary: System Reliability Engineering in the Age of Industry 4.0

## Thesis
Industry 4.0 necessitates a shift from static, periodic reliability analysis to dynamic, real-time Prognostics and Health Management (PHM) driven by cyber-physical systems (CPS), IoT, and AI.

## Key Findings
1.  **Event-Driven Efficiency:** Lebesgue Sampling (state-transition based) provides superior resource efficiency for edge devices compared to Riemann Sampling (time-based), maintaining predictive accuracy with reduced data volume.
2.  **Architectural Evolution:** Zonal ECU architectures reduce wiring complexity and latency compared to domain-based architectures, facilitating better scalability and local resource sharing.
3.  **Fault Tolerance Regimes:** Systems must implement fail-operational and fail-degraded strategies to ensure continuity in autonomous environments where human fallback is unavailable.
4.  **Trustworthy AI:** The integration of Explainable AI (XAI) and rigorous model verification is mandatory for managing rare, high-impact failure events in safety-critical systems.

## Truex Post-Gall Relevance (Grammar of Consequence)
*   **State:** Distributed swarm nodes operate in volatile, high-stakes environments with limited computational overhead.
*   **Event:** Integration of Lebesgue-based event-driven telemetry and zonal ECU architectures within the Truex substrate.
*   **Consequence:** Real-time adaptation to cascading failure modes and sustained fail-operational integrity without centralized overhead.
