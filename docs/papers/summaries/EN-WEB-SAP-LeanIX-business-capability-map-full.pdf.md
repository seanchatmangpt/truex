# Summary: SAP LeanIX Business Capability Map (Full Version)

**File:** EN-WEB-SAP-LeanIX-business-capability-map-full.pdf

## 1. Main Thesis
The SAP LeanIX Business Capability Map provides a comprehensive, three-level hierarchical taxonomy of enterprise capabilities. It serves as the foundational "Periodic Table" for Enterprise Architecture (EA), enabling organizations to map applications to business value, identify functional gaps, and provide a stable framework for strategic planning and modernization.

## 2. Key Findings
- **Capability Stratification:** Business functions are organized into high-level domains (e.g., Asset Management, Finance, R&D) and decomposed down to Level 3 granular capabilities (e.g., "Idea Selection and Execution", "Carbon Footprint Calculation").
- **Application Mapping:** By binding software applications to specific capabilities, the map exposes duplications (redundant systems) and "maverick" behaviors (functions performed without a governed system).
- **Foundational Blueprint:** The map acts as a "source of truth" for what a business *does*, independent of *how* it does it or *who* does it, providing a stable ontology for enterprise execution.

## 3. Truex Grammar of Consequence Analysis

### Substrate (The Enterprise Operating Environment)
The full capability map defines the **Operational Field**. It is the total landscape of possible business consequences.

### Membrane (The Capability Boundary)
Each named capability (e.g., "Supplier Invoice Processing") represents a **Consequence Membrane**. It defines the specific "Operating Law" that must be satisfied for a business attempt to become a legitimate consequence.

### Attempt (The Business Process Instance)
Any instance of a business action (e.g., a purchase order request) is an **Attempt** to move from one capability node to another.

### Admit / Refuse (The Governance Function)
The Business Capability Map provides the **Ontological Law** for admission. If an action does not map to a recognized capability, it is **Refused** as "out-of-bounds" or "shadow IT."

### Receipt (The Enterprise Record)
The mapping of an application to a capability creates a **Provenance Receipt**. It proves that a specific software consequence is authorized by the enterprise architecture.
