# Summary: SAP LeanIX Business Capability Map (Light Version)

**File:** EN-WEB-SAP-LeanIX-business-capability-map-light.pdf

## 1. Main Thesis
The "Light" version of the SAP LeanIX Business Capability Map provides a high-level (Level 1 and Level 2) overview of enterprise functions. It is designed for strategic communication and rapid alignment, offering a distilled view of the business architecture to facilitate decision-making without the "noise" of Level 3 granular details.

## 2. Key Findings
- **Structural Clarity:** Focuses on the core pillars of enterprise operation (Sales, Marketing, Supply Chain, etc.) to provide a "Executive Summary" of the organizational capability set.
- **Gap Identification:** Enables rapid identification of missing macro-level capabilities, such as a lack of "Sustainability Management" or "Cybersecurity" within a specific business unit.
- **Architectural On-ramp:** Serves as the initial entry point for organizations moving from high-entropy, unmapped processes toward a structured, EA-governed state.

## 3. Truex Grammar of Consequence Analysis

### Substrate (The Macro-Operating Field)
The Light Map defines the **High-Level Topology** of the substrate. It outlines the major "continents" of consequence where the business operates.

### Membrane (The Strategic Boundary)
The Level 1 and 2 domains act as **Strategic Membranes**. They provide the coarse-grained admission filters for large-scale enterprise motion.

### Attempt (The Strategic Initiative)
Strategic initiatives (e.g., "Digitizing Sourcing and Procurement") are high-level **Attempts** that must be admitted into the corporate substrate.

### Admit / Refuse (The Strategic Gate)
The Light Map provides the criteria for **Executive Admission (`μ_exec`)**. It answers whether a proposed initiative is "lawful" according to the organization's strategic intent.

### Receipt (The Business Case)
The admitted initiative, mapped to the Light Map, produces a **Strategic Receipt**, which is then decomposed into granular consequences in the Full Map.
