# Summary: FounderForce 90 Days (30-60-90 Day Plan Templates)

**File:** FounderForce 90 days.pdf

## 1. Main Thesis
The paper posits that the successful integration of a **Force Multiplier** (strategic partner) into a Principal's executive rhythm is a deterministic process governed by a rigorous 90-day onboarding framework. It argues that the first 90 days set the precedent for the entire partnership, moving from passive learning to active ownership of the "Top 20%" highest-impact responsibilities.

## 2. Key Findings
- **30 Days (Learning & Understanding Expectations):** Focuses on the "listening tour," auditing existing calendars, and documentation of all current systems. This is the observation phase.
- **60 Days (Manage Email, Calendar, & Communication):** Transitioning to active management of the communication arm. Establishing a "communication cadence" and "meeting model" to ensure alignment.
- **90 Days (Own Top 20% & Implementation):** Full ownership of core responsibilities. The Force Multiplier begins to optimize decision-making across the company and implements the strategic vision.
- **Systematic Documentation:** A recurring mandate across all phases is to "document all systems and processes," transforming implicit knowledge into explicit, executable laws.

## 3. Truex Grammar of Consequence Analysis

### Substrate (The Executive Rhythm)
The substrate comprises the Principal's raw schedule, recurring meetings, projects, and the "current rhythm of the business." It is the chaotic field of events that the Force Multiplier must organize.

### Membrane (The 30-60-90 Template)
The templates act as the **Computational Membrane**. They define the boundary of what is expected and the stages of admission for the new agent. They enforce the "Law" of the onboarding process.

### Attempt (The Listening Tour & Audits)
The "listening tour" and "calendar audit" are the initial **Attempts** to understand and mutate the system. They are exploratory motions designed to gather the state variables necessary for later execution.

### Admit / Refuse (Strategic Alignment)
The Principal and the Force Multiplier engage in a continuous cycle of **Admitting** behaviors that align with the "ideal daily schedule" and **Refusing** "maverick" tasks or inefficient "low-impact" projects. The audit process formally refuses legacy inefficiencies.

### Receipt (System Documentation)
The mandate to "document all systems and processes" is the creation of **Receipts**. These documents serve as the proof-bearing evidence of the "Law" of the position, ensuring that consequences (outputs) are repeatable and deterministic.
