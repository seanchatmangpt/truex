# Summary: FounderForce Force Multiplier Job Descriptions

**File:** FounderForce Job Desc.pdf

## 1. Main Thesis
The paper defines the specialized roles of **Force Multipliers** (Chief of Staff, Executive Assistant, Personal Assistant) as strategic "right hands" that expand a Founder's capacity. It argues that while titles vary, the core function is universal: acting as a highly aligned extension of the Principal to achieve greater organizational outcomes through delegated agency.

## 2. Key Findings
- **Chief of Staff (CoS):** A "future-oriented" agent that bridges gaps, manages long-term planning, and acts as a strategic advisor. The CoS is an information funnel and facilitator who manages "business rhythms."
- **Executive Assistant (EA):** A "now-oriented" agent focused on administrative tactical execution, communication hub management, and system implementation. The EA prioritizes the Executive's attention and "manages the Executive."
- **Personal Assistant (PA):** A "context-oriented" agent that manages the personal life and family logistics of the executive, ensuring that the Principal's private substrate is optimized for high-performance output.
- **Universal Force Multiplication:** Regardless of the specific domain (Future, Now, Personal), all roles are designed to multiply the impact of the Founder by absorbing deterministic workload.

## 3. Truex Grammar of Consequence Analysis

### Substrate (Founder Capacity)
The substrate is the Founder's finite time, energy, and decision-making bandwidth. It is the resource pool that the Force Multipliers seek to expand.

### Membrane (The Job Description as Constitutional Law)
The Job Description serves as the **Active Computational Membrane**. It defines the "Law" (μ) for each agent type. It specifies which types of system motions are within the agent's jurisdiction (e.g., CoS manages "future," EA manages "now").

### Attempt (Delegated Actions)
Every email sent, project facilitated, or property managed is an **Attempt** by the Force Multiplier to mutate the state of the Founder's world. These motions are performed on behalf of the Founder's intent.

### Admit / Refuse (The Information Funnel)
The Chief of Staff and Executive Assistant act as the **Deterministic Admission Function**. They **Admit** high-priority information and projects to the Founder's attention and **Refuse** (filter) noise, low-impact requests, and distractions. This is the "information funnel" identified in the CoS description.

### Receipt (Executed Action Items & Records)
The "action items executed," "meeting minutes," and "reports prepared" are the **Receipted Facts** of the agent's operation. They provide proof that the delegated intent has been successfully converted into consequence.
