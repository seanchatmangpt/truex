# Product Thesis: Hierarchical Decomposition for Deterministic Process Projection

The research in **"Hierarchical Decomposition of Separable Workflow-Nets"** manufactures an algorithmic boundary law for the automated transformation of sound Workflow-Nets (WF-nets) into Partially Ordered Workflow Language (POWL) 2.0. This **Projection** ensures that complex process behaviors in the **Substrate** are recursively decomposed into sound, hierarchical structures that are **Admitted** by construction.

## Accounting Domains (Conserved Authority)

1.  **Soundness (W1):** Conserved through the identification of safe and sound SESE (Single-Entry-Single-Exit) fragments.
2.  **Separability (W2):** Defined by the hierarchical nesting of state machine (Choice) and marked graph (Concurrency) templates.
3.  **Language Preservation (T1):** The algorithm **Promotes** the exact behavior of the input WF-net into the POWL model, ensuring no **Attempts** are lost or added.
4.  **Structural Duality (T2):** Exploits the duality between Choice Graphs (generalized decisions) and Partial Orders (generalized concurrency).

## Key Findings (Projections of Authority)

-   **Recursive Partitioning Membrane:** The algorithm recursively **Attempts** to partition the WF-net by "hiding conflicts" (to find partial orders) or "hiding concurrency" (to find choice graphs).
-   **POWL 2.0 Promotion:** Successfully **Promotes** non-block-structured behavior—such as unstructured loops and concurrent jumps—into a unified hierarchical representation.
-   **Completeness on Separable Nets:** Proves that all "separable" WF-nets (free from PT-handles and TP-handles) can be fully **Admitted** into the POWL hierarchy.
-   **Scalability Benchmarks:** Demonstrated 100% success rate and superior performance on 1,493 industrial/synthetic models, justifying its use as a core **Projection** engine.

## Relevance to Truex Post-Gall Operating Substrate

-   **POWL VKG Foundation:** Provides the formal grounding for the **POWL VKG Hook**, allowing the **Membrane** to recursively decompose and verify complex **Substrate** behaviors.
-   **Refusal of Overlaps:** The "separable" requirement acts as a **Refusal Condition** for processes with unstructured overlaps (handles) that threaten deterministic authority.
