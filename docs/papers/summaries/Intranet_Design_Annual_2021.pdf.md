# Summary: Intranet Design Annual 2021 (Nielsen Norman Group)

## Thesis
This report identifies the architectural **States** and **Events** that defined the world's most effective intranets during the 2020-2021 global pandemic. It argues that the intranet has transitioned from a static repository to a dynamic, Agile-driven "Source" of truth, serving as the central nervous system for distributed workforces. The thesis posits that rapid, user-centric design iterations are the primary consequence of extreme environmental stress (COVID-19).

## Key Findings (States, Events, Consequences)

### 1. The State of Distributed Resilience
*   **State:** A global pandemic forces an immediate transition to remote-first or hybrid work.
*   **Event:** Deployment of a centralized "COVID-19 Resource Hub" within the intranet.
*   **Consequence:** The intranet becomes the primary anchor for organizational stability, ensuring all agents (employees) operate on synchronized, verified information.

### 2. The Acceleration Event (Agile Compression)
*   **State:** Traditional intranet development cycles averaged 3.3 years.
*   **Event:** Adoption of Agile, Lean, and Design Thinking methodologies.
*   **Consequence:** Development timeframes collapsed to an average of 17 months, with some winning designs (e.g., Keysight Pulse) completed in just over a year, demonstrating that capability manufacturing can be accelerated by environmental necessity.

### 3. The State of Capability Mapping
*   **State:** Organizations with high-complexity capability needs (Energy, Finance, Tech).
*   **Event:** Integration of personalized homepages, powerful people search, and real-time chat.
*   **Consequence:** Enhanced "Capability Discovery" across the substrate, allowing contingent and full-time agents to identify resources and expertise without hierarchical latency.

### 4. The Governance Consequence
*   **State:** Content fragmentation and "information rot" over time.
*   **Event:** Implementation of built-in, systemic governance (e.g., ConocoPhillips' scalable platform).
*   **Consequence:** A self-cleaning infostructure that maintains technical and semantic integrity through automated or semi-automated verification cycles.
