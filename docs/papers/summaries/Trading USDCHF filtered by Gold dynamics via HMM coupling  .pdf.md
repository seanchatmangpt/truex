# Summary: Trading USDCHF filtered by Gold dynamics via HMM coupling (Trading USDCHF filtered by Gold dynamics via HMM coupling  .pdf)

## 1. Main Thesis
The paper proposes a quantitative trading strategy for the USDCHF currency pair that leverages the intermarket correlation between USDCHF and Gold. By using a Coupled Hidden Markov Model (CHMM), the strategy filters trading signals for USDCHF based on the state dynamics of Gold. This approach aims to capture the underlying causal relationships between these assets and overcome the problem of lag inherent in traditional technical indicators, thereby improving predictive accuracy and risk-adjusted returns.

## 2. Key Findings
- **Causal Asset Coupling:** CHMM effectively models the negative correlation between USDCHF and Gold, allowing the state of one asset to inform the probability transitions of the other.
- **State-Based Prediction:** The model successfully identifies hidden market states (e.g., bull, bear, random) and uses the Viterbi algorithm to predict the next most probable state and observation.
- **Indicator Transformation:** Using RSI and CCI as observations for the HMM, rather than raw price data, addresses the challenges of non-stationarity and ensures that inputs from different markets have a comparable scale.
- **Superior Risk Management:** Strategies employing dynamic allocation based on CHMM state probabilities showed significant improvements in the Sharpe ratio and reduced volatility compared to standard technical strategies.
- **Lag Mitigation:** The Markov property of the HMM provides a more "immediate" signal by making transitions dependent only on the current state, effectively removing the historical lag that plagues many oscillators.
- **Strategy Performance:** All four CHMM-based strategies outperformed their standard counterparts in backtesting, with the "CCI with dynamic allocation" variant being particularly profitable.

## 3. Truex Grammar of Consequence Analysis

### Substrate
The global financial markets, specifically the state-space of currency (USDCHF) and commodity (Gold) pairs.

### Membrane
The **Coupled Hidden Markov Model (CHMM)** and the **Viterbi Algorithm** act as the **Membrane**. They define the logical boundary where high-entropy market noise is transformed into "admissible" trading states.

### Attempt
Each predicted observation (e.g., RSI cross-over) generated by the model represents an **Attempt** to mutate the capital state of the substrate (i.e., execute a trade).

### Admit / Refuse
The system **Admits** a trade only if it satisfies the "Operating Law" defined by the CHMM's transition probabilities and the predefined trigger rules (e.g., SMA cross-over). Inconsistent or low-probability signals are terminaly **Refused** to prevent capital depletion.

### Receipt
The **Trade Execution Log**, characterized by its deterministic entry/exit points and its association with a specific HMM state transition, serves as the **Receipt** proving the validity of the market assertion.
