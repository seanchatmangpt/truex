# Summary: Intermarket Trading Strategies (Markos Katsanos)

## Thesis
The central premise of Katsanos' work is that the global financial ecosystem is a singular, interconnected organism where money flow between asset classes (stocks, bonds, commodities, and currencies) creates predictable **States** of lead-lag relationships. Intermarket analysis, driven by statistical correlation and regression, is the primary mechanism for identifying these states to gain a predictive advantage over traditional, single-market technical indicators.

## Key Findings (States, Events, Consequences)

### 1. The Intermarket State of Interconnectivity
*   **State:** Globalization has integrated international markets into a converged network.
*   **Event:** A shift in one major market (e.g., Bank of Japan buying dollars to push the Yen down).
*   **Consequence:** A chain reaction across the network (e.g., Nikkei rise -> Asian markets rise -> European markets rise -> US index futures rise -> "Yen carry trade" encourages buying US bonds/equities).

### 2. The Predictive State of Bonds
*   **State:** Bonds traditionally act as a leading indicator for the broader economy.
*   **Event:** Bonds peak or bottom during an economic cycle.
*   **Consequence:** Stocks and commodities follow with a significant lag (typically 12-24 months for stocks), providing an early warning system for recession or recovery.

### 3. The State of Uncorrelated Diversification
*   **State:** Portfolio volatility is a function of asset correlation.
*   **Event:** Inclusion of uncorrelated or negatively correlated assets (commodities/forex) into a 100% equity portfolio.
*   **Consequence:** Drastic reduction in standard deviation (risk) while maintaining or improving returns (e.g., a 64% risk reduction in tested models).

### 4. The Divergence Event
*   **State:** Two highly correlated markets move in tandem.
*   **Event:** A decoupling occurs where one market fails to follow the other (Divergence).
*   **Consequence:** The lagging market is expected to "catch up" or a trend reversal is imminent, triggering high-probability trading signals.

### 5. The Neural Network State
*   **State:** Financial time series are inherently non-linear and non-stationary.
*   **Event:** Deployment of Artificial Neural Networks (ANN) or Support Vector Machines (SVM).
*   **Consequence:** Superior modeling of non-linear relationships and higher trade turnover compared to rigid rule-based systems, though at the cost of interpretability ("black box").
