## 1. Gall as Sequencing Law
Gall establishes that complex systems cannot be designed top-down; they must emerge from working simple systems. In Truex, Gall dictates that we never scale unstable hooks. S0 (Receipts) must be proven before S1 (Replay), S1 before S2 (Engine), and S2 before S3 (SQLite Persistence). Abstractions that expand faster than their replay proof are identified as **unlawful architecture drift**.

## 2. DFLSS as Capability Law
Truex moves beyond simple benchmarking into operational capability measurement. DFLSS (Design for Lean Six Sigma) frames Truex metrics around CTQs (Critical-To-Quality) such as *Replay Convergence %* and *Refusal Correctness*. Rather than asking "how fast?", the operational law asks "what is the capability of the system under adversarial stress?" accounting for factors like poison rates, match ratios, and restart budgets.

## 3. TPS as Runtime Control Law
Truex operates as a Toyota Production System (TPS) software manufacturing line.
- **Jidoka (Autonomation):** Refusal of unlawful mutations acts as the Andon line stop.
- **Poka-yoke:** Strict condition evaluation prevents defective operations.
- **Kanban:** Lazy, JIT hook evaluations prevent *Muda* (processing waste).
- **Heijunka:** The system avoids *Muri* (overburden) through capacity refusal.
- **Genchi Genbutsu:** The Observatory acts as the live factory floor visualization.

## 4. OTP as Operational Topology
OTP equivalence provides the spatial geometry for the runtime. Truex Hooks operate within a deterministic actor model:
- **Isolated Process State** ensures memory corruption is localized.
- **FIFO Mailboxes** provide temporal ordering constraints essential for replay.
- **Supervision Trees** dictate deterministic restart budgets and poison message quarantine.
The topology proves that the runtime governs *ongoing operational organisms*, not just stateless reactions.

## 5. VKG as Projection Geometry
The Virtual Knowledge Graph (VKG) moves Truex from "one shared database state" to "observer-relative operational projection." It creates a reality field where consequence is navigable based on the observer's authority and receipt lineage. VKG collapses apps, workflows, and audit logs into a single *receipted operational field with many lawful projections*.

## 6. Replay as Trust Law
Replay is the ultimate arbiter of operational truth. Without deterministic convergence upon replay, a receipt is meaningless. Replay proves that the same input + same law + same entropy context = same consequence.

## 7. Observability as Production Law
The Observatory is not documentation; it is the sensory cortex of the system. Observability generates *topology-bearing telemetry*. By extracting AST and real-time persistence facts into OCEL/POWL models, the Observatory provides the environmental feedback loop required for autonomic agent behavioral adaptation.

## 8. Accounting as Operational Epistemology
Accounting in Truex is strictly divided into domains (message lifecycle, process lifecycle, command execution, agent run). *Quarantine* and *Restart* are not system failures; they are healthy operational accounting states proving the Jidoka and Supervisor mechanisms are active. Truex establishes that *receipts outrank assertions*.

## 9. Entropy Mismatch as the Current Frontier
The boundary of the current architecture lies in managing non-deterministic entropy (clocks, UUIDs, asynchronous context bleed). Any ambient entropy that leaks into a hook execution destroys the Replay Law. The active frontier is ensuring 100% seeded, deterministic inputs are preserved across all actor boundaries.

## 10. Next Gall Layers
The next layers of system scaling must focus on *Cross-Hook Topology*: cascading rollback divergence, recursive hook storms, and cross-projection consistency within the VKG. Distributed OTP/network topologies must NOT be built until local single-process topological physics are mathematically flawless under maximal stress.
