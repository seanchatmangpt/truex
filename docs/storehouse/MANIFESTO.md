# Storehouse Infrastructure Manifesto: From Donation to Stewardship Receipting

## The Shift
The legacy model of "Donation" is broken. It is a one-way, high-entropy dump that strips the receiver of dignity and the giver of accountability. We are transitioning to **Stewardship Receipting**.

### 1. Donation vs. Stewardship Receipting
- **Donation:** A tax-advantaged disposal of surplus. No guarantee of utility. No closed-loop feedback.
- **Stewardship Receipting:** A cryptographic commitment of resources into a shared inventory. Every unit is tracked from the moment of "gleaning" to the moment of "provisioning." The "Receipt" is not just for taxes; it is a proof of impact.

## Core Pillars

### Gleaning as the Product Wedge
The primary entry point into the Storehouse is the **Gleaning / Overflow Logic**. We don't wait for "charity"; we harvest the systemic overflow of the industrial food and supply chains. Gleaning is the act of capturing value that the market has abandoned but human need still requires.

### Provision-Routing Architecture
Provision is **incomplete** until it reaches its appointed receiver. A resource sitting in a warehouse is not a provision; it is a liability. Our architecture ensures that routing is "intent-aware," matching the specific needs of Field Wallets with the available inventory on Overflow Rails.

### Physical Primitives
We manifest this digital coordination through physical touchpoints:
- **Field Cards:** Localized identifiers for resource requests.
- **Edge Cards:** Verification tokens for logistics nodes.
- **Overflow Rails:** Dynamic supply lines that move gleaned goods.
- **Field Wallets:** The receiver's interface for claiming and receipting provision.

## The Goal
To build a world where "surplus" is impossible because the Storehouse is always watching, always routing, and always receipting the fulfillment of human needs.
