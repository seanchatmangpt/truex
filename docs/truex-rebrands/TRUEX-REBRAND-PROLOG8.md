## 2. ORIGINAL PURPOSE
Imperative languages (JS, Python) are terrible for expressing complex business rules (e.g., "A customer can only checkout if they have a valid address AND no outstanding invoices"). `prolog8` was built to provide a high-speed, declarative logic engine (inspired by Prolog) that could evaluate deeply nested operational constraints and Horn clauses efficiently.
