## 2. ORIGINAL PURPOSE
You can't test a highly complex, multi-agent operational workflow in production. You need a safe environment that perfectly mirrors production physics but allows for time-travel and destructive testing. `sos` (Simulation OS) was built to act as the autonomic sandbox where `ggen` outputs could be booted, simulated, subjected to chaos testing (via `bullshit_scanner.sh` and `astar`), and then verified for topological closure before ever touching a live database.
