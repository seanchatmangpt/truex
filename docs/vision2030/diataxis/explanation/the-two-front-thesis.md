> **Objective:** Understanding the dual nature of the Vision 2030 architecture—as an enterprise execution substrate, and as a human-facing stewardship infrastructure.

## 1. The Theological and Architectural Bridge

The book *0 to 10%* is not merely content; it is the pastoral explanation layer for an infrastructure substrate. 

The software is the plow. The receipt is the harvest record.

The Truex Execution Trust loop maps perfectly to the biblical Storehouse Stewardship loop:

`attempt → hook → admission/refusal → durable motion → receipt → replay → accounting → promotion`

## 2. Category Lock: Storehouse Infrastructure

This is not church fintech, donor management, or charity routing. Donation systems prove transfer; **Storehouse systems prove consequence.**

A donation receipt says money moved. A stewardship receipt says *what the money became*.

The theological unlock is moving away from guilt-based, scarcity-driven tithing math (`How do I force 10% out of scarcity?`) to field-based stewardship (`What field has God entrusted me to cultivate, and what increase can I joyfully return the first tenth from?`). 

The first portion is the receipt that the increase remains under God. It prevents both bursting nets and buried talents.
